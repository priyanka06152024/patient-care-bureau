PATIENT CARE BUREAU WEBSITE
===========================

This is a responsive multi-page static website.

PAGES
- index.html: Home page with full-screen slider, services, process, sample testimonials, gallery preview and CTA
- about.html: About, story and values
- services.html: Service details and interactive enquiry cart
- gallery.html: Gallery layout
- contact.html: Contact form that opens WhatsApp and embedded Google map
- assets/style.css: Responsive styling and animations
- assets/script.js: Slider, mobile navigation, scroll reveals, WhatsApp enquiry and service cart

HOW TO PREVIEW
1. Extract the ZIP folder.
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox.
3. Internet is needed for Google Fonts, Lucide icons, stock photos and the embedded Google Map.

BEFORE PUBLISHING
- Gallery photos are illustrative stock images. Replace with real, approved business photos.
- Homepage testimonials are sample layout text, not verified customer reviews. Replace them with genuine reviews or remove that section.
- Confirm service availability, staff qualifications, pricing, hours and exact service scope before publishing.
- The contact form and service enquiry cart open WhatsApp. They do not store submissions or process payments.
- Check that the phone numbers, email addresses, map pin and address are correct.
- Add your hosting/domain details and privacy policy before collecting any personal information.

The enquiry cart is an enquiry shortlist, not a payment checkout cart.
